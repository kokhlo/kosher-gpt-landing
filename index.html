May one eat chicken with milk?
KosherGPT

No. Eating poultry with milk is forbidden by rabbinic decree, though not by the Torah.

Unlike meat with milk, cooking poultry with milk or benefiting from it is not forbidden; only eating it is.

Sources
1
Mishneh Torah, Forbidden Foods 9The Rambam on meat and milk, poultry included.Open in Sefaria
2
Shulchan Arukh, Yoreh De'ah 87Which meat and milk are forbidden to cook, eat and benefit from.Open in Sefaria
How long after meat before dairy?
KosherGPT

The common practice is to wait six hours.

Some communities keep shorter customs, recorded by the Rema. Ask your rabbi which custom your family follows.

Sources
1
Mishneh Torah, Forbidden Foods 9:28Waiting after meat before eating dairy.Open in Sefaria
2
Shulchan Arukh, Yoreh De'ah 89:1Six hours, with the Rema on other customs.Open in Sefaria
